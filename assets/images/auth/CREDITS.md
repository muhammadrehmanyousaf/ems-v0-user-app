## Why these three

They are **venues and decor, not brides**. Three reasons, in order:

1. **A marketplace sells the event, not a person.** A bride portrait makes us
   look like a bridal-wear label. An arched hall full of chandeliers makes us
   look like the place you book it.
2. **Cultural safety.** The strongest portrait candidates in the pool were
   Indian bridal shoots carrying sindoor and bindi. On a Pakistani product that
   is not a small slip — it is the kind of thing a customer screenshots. Rooms
   carry none of that risk.
3. **`hall-arches` rhymes with the brand.** It is a colonnade of receding
   arches under crystal — the Mehrab motif of our own design system, in real
   architecture. We did not draw that arch onto a photograph; we found a
   building that already had it.

All three are shot dark and warm, which is what lets ivory type sit on them
without a heavy scrim fighting the image.

## Resolution

1080 × 1560, quality-compressed at source (~250 KB each, 740 KB total). The
1.4× overscan on height is deliberate: `AuthScene` drifts the image by 14 px and
scales it to 1.12 over fourteen seconds, so a screen-exact crop would show the
edge.
